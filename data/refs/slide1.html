Chandausi is where NAKSHA launched.
AI finds 5,488 buildings there. The municipal record has 6.

KSHETRA harmonizes the ten disagreeing datasets a city holds into one cadastral layer, and attaches an honest confidence to every decision — so an officer reviews only what is genuinely uncertain.

Legacy sheets carry 3–4 m error against orthoimagery specified to 10 cm, so overlaying them is meaningless. We georeference first, match on geometry, and repair topology without losing land.

What makes it different

  • It closes the loop. NAKSHA's own review names ground truthing its biggest lagging component — so KSHETRA computes where to send the next surveyor, by submodular optimisation over a GP error field.
  • Its confidence is calibrated, not scored. When it says 96%, 96% of those are right — and the reliability curve is in the app.
  • It refuses to guess. Parcel-sized gaps are flagged for survey, never filled. Filling them would fabricate a boundary.
  • It proves no land was lost. A double-entry area ledger reconciles every square metre through the pipeline.
